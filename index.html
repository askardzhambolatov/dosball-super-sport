Welcome to DOSBALL

DOSBALL is an innovative international Super Sport created as a new sports discipline and global ecosystem combining athletic competition, video gaming, clubs, professional leagues, education and international development.

New Sport

DOSBALL combines dynamic elements inspired by basketball, volleyball, handball, rugby and football into a new competitive sport.

International Federation

The DOSBALL Federation develops official rules, clubs, tournaments, championships and international cooperation.

Digital Ecosystem

DOSBALL connects the physical sport with a video game, memberships, referrals, digital registration and future online services.

Global Mission

The long-term mission is to develop DOSBALL worldwide, build professional leagues and work toward international recognition.

DOSBALL Sport

Fast, energetic and designed for international competition.

Teams

6 athletes play on the court with fast substitutions.

Match

Three periods of 20 minutes of clean playing time.

Scoring

A fist strike through the ring scores 2 points. A hand throw through the ring scores 1 point.

Three Rings

Each side of the court uses three vertical DOSBALL rings.

Schools, Colleges & Universities

DOSBALL supports cooperation with educational institutions around the world.

Educational Use Policy

General education schools, colleges, universities and other educational institutions under the authority of any state or country may use the official DOSBALL game rules free of charge after entering into a digital agreement with the DOSBALL Federation.

The agreement may be concluded for an indefinite term for educational and non-commercial use.

Educational institutions receiving this free right may use DOSBALL within physical education classes, educational programs and approved non-commercial activities.

This free educational right does not grant the institution the right to independently organize paid DOSBALL sections, paid commercial training programs, sell DOSBALL franchises or sublicense DOSBALL intellectual property.

Commercial DOSBALL sections and paid official training programs may only be organized by the DOSBALL Federation or by persons and organizations specifically authorized by the Federation.

Social Responsibility

DOSBALL is designed not only as a sport and business ecosystem, but also as a platform for positive social development.

Families

Social programs may support athletes and their families.

Youth

DOSBALL promotes healthy activity, discipline and teamwork.

Community

Federation projects may cooperate with charitable and social organizations.

Sport Integrity

DOSBALL must never be used for sports betting, gambling, wagering, lotteries or gambling-related activities.

The DOSBALL ecosystem is intended for sport, education, healthy competition, community development and legitimate business activity.
