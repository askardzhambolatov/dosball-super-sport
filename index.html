What is DOSBALL?

DOSBALL is a completely new hybrid team sport created in 2006 by Askar Jambulatov. It combines dynamic elements inspired by basketball, volleyball, handball, rugby and football in one spectacular competition, while building a worldwide ecosystem of athletes, clubs, professional leagues, education, video gaming and franchise development.

Global Mission

Inspire boys and men to choose active sport, discipline, teamwork and real-world community while building a new international sporting legacy.

Future of Global Sport

DOSBALL is designed for worldwide development through authorized clubs, championships, regional leadership, professional leagues and international cooperation.

More Than a Sport

The project connects physical sport, a video game, membership, referrals, clubs, franchises, athlete careers, education and social responsibility.

DOSBALL Sport

Fast, physical and distinctive: a basketball-size court, vertical rings, a compact bouncing ball and three different periods of play.

6players on court
3 × 20minutes clean time
3vertical rings per side
25athletes in roster
27official rules

Three Periods

Period 1: athletic shoes. Period 2: roller skates / roller boots. Period 3: athletic shoes again. Fast hockey-style substitutions are used.

Scoring

A fist strike through a ring scores 2 points. A hand throw through a ring scores 1 point. Knee strikes are also part of DOSBALL technique.

Three-Ring Goal

Each side uses three vertical illuminated rings: one central ring and two side rings connected to it and turned inward at approximately 45 degrees.

Overtime

Three players take three attempts: a throw from 6 metres, a fist strike and a knee strike.

Authorized DOSBALL Clubs

Clubs are the physical heart of DOSBALL: training, athlete development, official competition and long-term community participation.

Club Training

Standard program: $30 per month for 12 training sessions, each approximately two hours, subject to local law and the authorized club agreement.

Club Capacity

With Federation approval, expanded DOSBALL club formats, including large hangar-type facilities, may be approved for up to 600 athletes.

Family Competition

DOSBALL also provides a path for family teams and recognizes first, second and third places in approved competitions.

Referral & Rewards Program

A transparent rewards model for active registered members who personally introduce new participants into legitimate DOSBALL products and services. It is not a gambling or lottery system.

$2 Video Game Reward

For each referred user's paid monthly DOSBALL video-game access, the eligible inviter may receive $2 for that paid month under the official program terms.

$3 Club Reward

If the referred person becomes an authorized DOSBALL Club member and pays the $30 monthly training fee, the eligible inviter may receive $3 for each paid month while that member continues official club training.

10% Franchise Reward

Where the official conditions are met, including the champion/franchise qualification path, an eligible inviter may receive 10% of the applicable franchise purchase payment under the Federation agreement.

Annual Referral Limit

The program is designed with a limit of no more than 1,000 new referred users per registered member per year, subject to verification and applicable law.

Reward integrity. Eligible rewards should be recorded transparently and paid on time under the official digital system. The program is based on real paid DOSBALL products/services and must not be presented as MLM, investment returns, wagering or a game of chance.

Academy of Succession

DOSBALL is designed to give successful athletes a future beyond the playing court.

1st–3rd Place Career Entry

Athletes who place first, second or third at approved competition levels can enter the DOSBALL career-development path.

Career Growth

The long-term path can progress from club athlete and champion to club leadership, International Regional Director of DOSBALL Clubs and possible Federation Council participation.

Champion Franchise Benefit

Athletes who achieve qualifying top international status may receive a 50% discount on eligible Federation franchise fees under the official program conditions.

Family & Succession Principle

DOSBALL intends to protect earned, verified member rewards and develop lawful succession/inheritance procedures for eligible accounts, subject to applicable law and final Federation terms.

DOSBALL Franchise

A sports franchise ecosystem for entrepreneurs and athletes who want to operate authorized DOSBALL clubs under Federation standards.

Official License

The franchise grants contractual rights to operate authorized DOSBALL activities for the agreed territory and term. When the license expires, official paid DOSBALL training must stop until the license is renewed.

Growth After Two Years

After two years of successful joint operation and compliance, a franchisee may qualify to acquire up to five DOSBALL club franchises, subject to Federation approval and contract.

Indicative Club Distribution

A current development model provides, after applicable taxes and approved expenses: 60% of net profit to the franchise operator, 20% to two coaches and 20% to the Federation. Final terms depend on the signed agreement and local law.

Separate Payment

Franchise payments are separate from the $11 monthly video-game subscription and from ordinary club training payments.

Schools, Colleges & Universities

DOSBALL supports cooperation with public and private educational institutions as a route to physical activity, teamwork and youth development.

Educational Use Policy. Schools, colleges and universities may receive free educational-use rights under a digital agreement with the DOSBALL Federation for non-commercial physical-education and approved educational activities. This does not authorize paid DOSBALL sections, commercial training, sublicensing, franchise sales or public redistribution of the complete official competition rulebook. Paid official training and commercial DOSBALL activities require Federation authorization.
