SUPER SPORT INTERNATIONAL

DOSBALL brings Sport, Society, State and Business together for the benefit of society.

A global sports ecosystem built around a new team sport, a video game, clubs, leagues, athlete development, entrepreneurship and social responsibility.

Digital-first roadmap: video game → federation → clubs → leagues → international competitions.

THE SPORT

A fast hybrid team sport

Teams

6 players on court with rolling substitutions and a 25-player roster concept.

Format

Three 20-minute clean-time periods. Roller skates are used in the second period.

Scoring

Players score through three vertical rings using throws, fist strikes and other permitted techniques.

ACADEMY OF SUCCESSION

From player to international leadership

Athletes who achieve competitive results can move through a structured career pathway: club player, champion, mentor, coach, club leader, regional director and international federation leadership.

Premier League

Professional competitive pathway.

Advanced national and regional competition.

Super National League

Top national level feeding international competition.

International competitions are envisioned every two years, hosted in different countries to expand global awareness.

SOCIAL IMPACT

Transparent, voluntary and community-focused

Charity

Voluntary and transparent support for athletes' families, children, widows and low-income communities through approved charitable cooperation.

Education

Cooperation proposals for schools, colleges and universities, including rules, classes and extracurricular programs.

Succession

Long-term member continuity and inheritance principles can be defined for eligible accounts, subject to legal review and federation policy.

Zero gambling policy

DOSBALL must not be used for sports betting, wagering, gambling, lotteries or gambling-related activity.
